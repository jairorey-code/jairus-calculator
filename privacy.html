Privacy Policy

Jairus Calculator · effective September 28, 2026

The short version

Jairus Calculator does not collect, store, share or sell any information about you. It has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. It never connects to the internet.

What stays on your iPhone

To remember your work between sessions, the app saves your calculation history (up to your last 80 calculations), your last answer, your degree/radian setting and your Jairus sheet lines. These are kept only on your device, inside the app's own storage. They are never sent anywhere. Deleting the app deletes them. If you back up your iPhone, they may be included in your own iCloud or computer backup, just like any other app's data. That backup belongs to you and is handled by Apple.

Children

The app is suitable for all ages and collects no information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
