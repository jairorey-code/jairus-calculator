Jairus Calculator

The JR-7 scientific calculator, with Jairus, a live math sheet, one turn away.

Getting around

Hold the phone upright for the JR-7 calculator. Turn it sideways for Jairus, where every line solves itself as you type. If rotation lock is on, tap Jairus at the top of the calculator, or ‹ JR-7 on the sheet's keypad.

Tips

  • Fractions: n/d builds a fraction; 2nd n/d makes a mixed number; ◂▸ flips an answer between fraction and decimal.
  • 2nd functions are printed in green above each key. Tap 2nd first.
  • History: the arrow pad's ▲ ▼ scroll back through your last 80 calculations.
  • Copy an answer: press and hold the display (calculator) or the answer (Jairus).
  • Jairus: define things and reuse them (a = 3, f(x) = x² + 1, f(a)). ans is the line above's answer; on the first line, it's the calculator's last answer.
  • Delete a line: press ⌫ on an empty line.
